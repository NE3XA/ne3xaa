School Management

One system for admissions, fees, results and communication

Schools spend too much time moving data between spreadsheets, paper registers and disconnected tools. Our school management system — powered by NE3XA Analytics — keeps student information, attendance, examinations and fee records in one place.

  • Student and teacher profiles with role-based access
  • Attendance, timetables and class management
  • Exams, results and printable report cards
  • Fees tracking and parent notifications
  • NE3XA Analytics: track records, generate insights and print reports
